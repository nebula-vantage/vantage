What is Vantage?

Vantage is a pickleball video analysis app in development for iPhone. It uses footage from a single camera to track players and the ball, so you can review your game with a little more perspective.

Do I need special equipment?

Start with a phone and a steady place to record. Keep the full court and players in view, then import the saved video into Vantage on your iPhone. A tripod can help, but you don’t need a dedicated camera system.

Is the app available yet?

Vantage is still in development. Sign up for early access to hear about opportunities to try it and help shape what comes next.

What can I see in my game?

The current iPhone app offers player and ball overlays, a ball trail, slow-motion replay, and frame-by-frame playback. We’re exploring more match insights as Vantage develops.

Which sports is it for?

We’re starting with pickleball and building around the way people actually play. Other racket sports are part of the bigger picture, but pickleball is our focus today.
